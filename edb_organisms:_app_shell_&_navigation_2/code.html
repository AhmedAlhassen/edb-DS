04 RTL Guidelines: Layout & Content Logic

Adapting interfaces for Arabic requires more than simple translation. These guidelines ensure the EDB digital experience feels native to RTL (Right-to-Left) users while maintaining technical integrity for mixed data like IBANs and Swift codes.

02 Arabic Typography (Noto Sans)

Latin Base (Inter)

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.

Leading: Normal (1.5)

Arabic (Noto Sans Arabic)

الثعلب البني السريع يقفز فوق الكلب الكسول.

Leading: Relaxed (1.8)
info

Vertical Rhythm Adjustment: Arabic glyphs have greater vertical reach (ascenders/descenders). To maintain the same visual density as Inter at 16px, Noto Sans Arabic must use a minimum line-height of 1.8 compared to the standard 1.5.

Usage: Standard body text, descriptive labels, and formal correspondence in Arabic documents.

Compliance with these RTL guidelines is mandatory for all Phase 5 developments. Developers should use logical properties (e.g., margin-inline-start instead of margin-left) to ensure these behaviors are inherited automatically from the global dir attribute.
